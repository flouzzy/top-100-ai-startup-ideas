# Stratospheric Aerosol Sim

> **Executive Summary:** A B2G / B2B solution targeting Governments, UN environmental agencies, climate reinsurance consortia to solve the following problem: Solar geo-engineering (such as stratospheric aerosol injection to cool the Earth) is considered by some states, but side effects (mould disturbance, destruction of the ozone layer) are unknown locally. Blinding risks causing climate wars. The approach relies on: A digital twin of the Earth's ultra-high resolution atmosphere (Exascale Earth Model). It combines fluid thermodynamics with predictive AI models to accurately simulate (kilometre mesh) how local aerosol injections would impact regional precipitation regimes over 10 years.
