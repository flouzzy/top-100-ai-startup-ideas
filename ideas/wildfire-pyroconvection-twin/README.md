# Wildfire Pyroconvection Twin

> **Executive Summary:** A B2B / B2G solution targeting Forestry agencies, civil security, electricity operators, insurance companies (reinsurance) to solve the following problem: Current models do not predict the behaviour of megafires (sixth generation fires) because they ignore the microclimate created by the fire itself (pyroconvection). This results in unpredictable material and human losses, and insurance refuses to cover these risks without precise modelling. The approach relies on: Development of a Neural Physics Engine integrating computational fluid dynamics (CFD) in real time with physics-informed neuron networks (PINNs). The system modeled the complex interaction between topography, forest fuels and thermal plumes generating their own weather.
