# Deep Omic Clock

> **Executive Summary:** A B2B solution targeting Long-term clinics, life insurance companies, researchers in medicine (CMO, Actuaries). to solve the following problem: Chronological age is a bad indicator of risk. Current epigenetic clocks are one-dimensional and lack precision to target personalized clinical interventions or validate the effectiveness of anti-ageing therapies. The approach relies on: A deep multimodal learning model integrating epigenome, proteoma, microbiome and longitudinal clinical data to create a World Model of biological aging, capable of simulating the effect of generogenic molecules.
