# Ocean CO2 Electrodialysis

> **Executive Summary:** A B2B (Marchés du carbone) / B2G solution targeting Technology companies buying high quality carbon credits (Microsoft, Stripe), governments, and heavy industries seeking to offset their residual emissions. to solve the following problem: Direct Air Capture (DAC) technologies are extremely energy efficient because of the low CO2 concentration in the air (400 ppm). The ocean contains 150 times more CO2 per volume than air, but its extraction is historically expensive and complex. The approach relies on: Design of continuous-flow bipolar electrodialysis modules, integrated with existing infrastructures (desalination plants, offshore platforms), to acidify sea water locally, degass CO2 in an ultra-efficient way with advanced selective membranes, and then release neutralized water (combating ocean acidification).
