# Lunar Regolith Refinery Sim

> **Executive Summary:** A B2B solution targeting Space agencies (NASA, ESA), space mining startups, offshore construction companies to solve the following problem: Extraction of minerals and oxygen from lunar regolith is essential for long-term space exploration (ISRU - In-Situ Resource Utilization), but physical tests cost billions (prototype launches) and often fail due to the unforeseen behaviour of abrasive lunar dust in vacuum and in low gravity. The approach relies on: A multi-scale granular physics engine (Discrete Element Method) coupled with thermochemical models running on GPU. It simulates exactly how regolith will melt, agglomerate or fracture under lasers or electrolysis reactions under lunar conditions.
