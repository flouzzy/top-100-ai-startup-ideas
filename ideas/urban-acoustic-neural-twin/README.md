# Urban Acoustic Neural Twin

> **Executive Summary:** A B2G / B2B solution targeting Municipalities, urban planners, transport manufacturers (metros, trams), and real estate developers. to solve the following problem: Urban noise pollution is a major public health issue, reducing life expectancy and causing cardiovascular disease. Current acoustic simulations (Ray Tracing audio) take days of calculation, are limited to small areas and fail to model the complex sound reflection of new building materials. The approach relies on: A city-scale acoustic simulation engine (Neural Twin) using physics-informed neuron networks (PINNs) to solve the real-time wave equation. It integrates 3D urban geometry, material properties and dynamic traffic to simulate noise propagation in an interactive way.
