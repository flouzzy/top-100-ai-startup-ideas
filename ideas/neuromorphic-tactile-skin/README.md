# Neuromorphic Tactile Skin

> **Executive Summary:** A B2B solution targeting Manufacturers of humanoid robots (Tesla, Figure), automated logistics companies (Amazon), and advanced medical prosthetics designers. to solve the following problem: The current robotic hands lack fine dexterity because they rely mainly on computer vision, which suffers from occlusions. Existing tactile sensors generate volumes of continuous data that are too heavy to process in real time (latency) and are fragile. The approach relies on: Creation of a polymer "skin" incorporating thousands of micro-pressure and shear sensors, linked to a neuromorphic chip architecture (Spiking Neural Networks) that only transmits "events" of change (asynchronous), reducing latency to microsecond and energy consumption by 99%.
