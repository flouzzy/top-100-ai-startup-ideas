# Wildfire Neural Twin

> **Executive Summary:** A B2B / B2G solution targeting Government forest management agencies, utilities, and insurance companies specializing in climate risk. to solve the following problem: Current models for the spread of forest fires are slow, based on static data and difficult to model the extreme fire dynamics induced by microclimate. This leads to late evacuations, massive infrastructure losses and incalculable insurance premiums. The approach relies on: Creation of a Neural Physics Engine that ingest LIDAR telemetry data, infrared satellite imagery and IoT wind data in real time to simulate combustion thermodynamics and fluid dynamics at submetric resolution, thousands of times faster than traditional CFD (Computational Fluid Dynamics) models.
