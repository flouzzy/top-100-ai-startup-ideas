# Photonic Tensor Satellite

> **Executive Summary:** A B2B solution targeting LEO constellation operators (Starlink, Kuiper), geospatial intelligence agencies, and Earth observation data providers. to solve the following problem: LEO satellites capture petabytes of raw data (hyperspectral images, SAR) but are limited by downlink to return them to Earth. Treating this data on board with conventional GPUs requires too much power (Watts) and generates heat that is impossible to dissipate in space vacuum. The approach relies on: Development of photonic tensor cores. These chips calculate matrice multiplications for neuron networks (e.g. detection of anomalies, intelligent compression) at the speed of light, with almost zero energy and thermal consumption compared to traditional silicon.
