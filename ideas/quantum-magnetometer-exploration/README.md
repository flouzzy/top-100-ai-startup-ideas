# Quantum Magnetometer Exploration

> **Executive Summary:** A B2B solution targeting Critical resource mining companies (rare lands, lithium, cobalt), national geological institutes, and underwater exploration companies. to solve the following problem: Traditional mineral exploration depends on costly, environmentally destructive drilling and low resolution geophysical surveys. Discovering deep or hidden deposits under thick overburdens becomes exponentially more difficult and expensive, slowing the energy transition. The approach relies on: Development of quantum sensors with NV centres (Nitrogen-Vacancy) in diamond or alkaline vapours, mounted on drone fleets, capable of detecting tiny magnetic anomalies in the basement with unprecedented sensitivity and spatial resolution, coupled with geophysical reversal algorithms based on AI.
