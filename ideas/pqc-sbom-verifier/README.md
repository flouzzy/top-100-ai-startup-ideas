## 3. Problem & Target Market

- **Business Model:** B2B
- **Target Audience:** CISO, DevSecOps teams of critical infrastructures (Banks, Defense, Health) and software publishers.
- **Urgent Pain Point:** With the imminent arrival of quantum computers, current encryption algorithms (RSA, ECC) will become obsolete ("Q-Day"). The transition to post-quantum cryptography (PQC) is mandatory. However, companies do not know exactly where cryptographic vulnerabilities are hiding in their sprawling software supply chain (open source dependencies, third-party libraries), making remediation impossible.
