# Xenotransplantation Immune Sim

> **Executive Summary:** A B2B solution targeting Xenotransplantation companies, genomic publishing research institutes (CRISPR), research hospitals to solve the following problem: Genomic editing of animal organs (e.g. pig heart) for human transplantation faces the deadly barrier of hyperacute rejection and endogenous viruses. In-vivo tests are slow, expensive, ethically complex and do not allow thousands of combinations of genetic editing to be tested. The approach relies on: A multi-omical simulation platform and in-silico immunology. It models the cascade of human complement and the interaction of porcine antigens (PERVs, Alpha-gal) with the human immune system at the molecular level using graphical neuron (GNN) networks and molecular dynamics.
