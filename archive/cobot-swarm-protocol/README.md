## 3. Problem & Target Market

- **Business Model:** B2B
- **Target Audience:** Warehouse logistics (Amazon, Ocado), agile manufacturing (Automotive, Aerospace), and precision agriculture.
- **Urgent Pain Point:** Robot fleets (AMR, AGV, drones) from different manufacturers do not know how to communicate with each other or coordinate spatially in a fluid way without going through a heavy and rigid central server. Congestion, downtime and collision deadlocks in dynamic environments (with humans) are ruining the ROI of robotic investments.
