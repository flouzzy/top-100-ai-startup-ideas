## 3. Problem & Target Market

- **Business Model:** B2B
- **Target Audience:** Autonomous vehicle manufacturers (Waymo, Cruise, Tesla), LiDAR sensor manufacturers, automotive safety regulators.
- **Urgent Pain Point:** Autonomous driving systems accumulate billions of kilometers of "annoying" real data (driving on a straight motorway), but are sorely lacking data on "Corner Cases" (rare, dangerous and complex situations: e.g. child running behind a truck in the snow, sensor blinded by the sun). It is physically impossible to film all these scenarios in the real world without killing people.
