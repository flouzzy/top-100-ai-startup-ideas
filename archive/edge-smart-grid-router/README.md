## 3. Problem & Target Market

- **Business Model:** B2B
- **Target Audience:** Distribution network managers (DSO - Enedis, PG&E), microgrid operators (Microgrids) and flexibility aggregators.
- **Urgent Pain Point:** The massive integration of intermittent renewable energies (solar, wind) and the proliferation of electric vehicles (V2G) destabilize the frequency and voltage of local electricity networks. Traditional centralized architecture (SCADA) suffers from too high latency to respond to millisecond fluctuations at the edge of the network.
